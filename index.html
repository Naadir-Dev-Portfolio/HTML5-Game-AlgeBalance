🧮 Scratch-pad:

Algebraverse was created by Naadir in 2024 with the help of GPT-4o.

  • Hints & built-in calculator
  • Earn XP & coins, avoid running out of lives
  • No level labels—just keep solving!
  • Tech stack:
    • HTML5 & CSS3 (responsive, custom properties, animations)
    • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), no frameworks
    • math.js for CAS operations
    • Canvas 2D for animated bubble background
    • LocalStorage for persisting XP, coins, theme, and high score
    • Google Fonts (Manrope) for clean typography

Solve each problem in turn:

  • Type your answer(s) and click “Check”.
  • Use “Hint” for step guidance (costs XP).
  • Use the scratch-pad calculator for help.
  • Earn coins for correct answers, lose a life for mistakes.
  • Game ends when lives reach 0.
